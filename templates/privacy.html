Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data: basic information about how you interact with the app (e.g. pages visited).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to:

  • Provide and operate the Spendly service for your account.
  • Display your expense history, summaries, and breakdowns.
  • Authenticate you securely when you sign in.
  • Improve the app based on aggregate, anonymised usage patterns.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secure database. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using an industry-standard algorithm before being saved. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may use limited third-party services to operate the platform (e.g. hosting infrastructure). These providers are contractually obligated to handle your data securely and only for the purposes of providing their services to us. We do not integrate with external advertising or analytics platforms that track you across other websites.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the app. We take privacy seriously and will respond promptly.
